# CampHawk round 2: image prompts

**Where to run them:** **ChatGPT** (chatgpt.com, free) is the best fit for both directions. **Gemini**
(gemini.google.com, free) also works. Paste one prompt per chat message. Each prompt asks for one image.

**How to pick:** make 2–3 versions of each and keep the one where the "calm area" (marked below) really
is calm, with nothing busy where the headline will sit. Never pick a version with text, a watermark,
warped trees or extra tents.

**Where to put them:** upload the files to GitHub at
**https://github.com/TylerFlores1992/Tools/upload/ccr-7bbd906e-003dsw/public/private/camphawk/round2**
(choose "Commit directly to the `ccr-7bbd906e-003dsw` branch"). Name them exactly as listed below; PNG
or JPG are both fine. The folder sits behind the Private password, so nothing is public.

Do whichever direction you like first, or both. Even just A1 and B1 are enough to start.

## Direction A: "Golden hour" (photographic)

**A1: `a1-hero-wide`, landscape 3:2**
> A cinematic photograph for a website hero, landscape 3:2. Golden hour at a campsite high in the
> Sierra Nevada: a single canvas bell tent glowing warm from a lantern inside, pitched on a flat granite
> ledge among tall ponderosa pines. Beyond it, a deep valley of layered blue-green ridges with thin mist,
> lit by a low sun from the right. Warm amber light rakes across the tent and the tree trunks, and the
> forest shadows are deep pine green. The tent and the light sit in the right half of the frame. The left
> 40% is calm, dark, softly out-of-focus pine forest and shadow, with no bright spots, for headline text.
> Shot on 35mm film, Kodak Portra 400, natural color, gentle grain, shallow depth of field. No people, no
> text, no logos, no cars, no lens-flare starbursts, not oversaturated.

**A2: `a2-hero-tall`, portrait 2:3** (the phone version of A1)
> The same scene as a portrait 2:3 photograph for a phone screen: golden hour at a Sierra Nevada
> campsite, a single canvas bell tent glowing from a lantern on a granite ledge among tall ponderosa
> pines, a misty valley of layered ridges beyond. The tent sits in the lower third. The upper half is a
> calm, softly lit sky fading into dark pine silhouettes, with no bright spots, for headline text. 35mm
> film look, Kodak Portra 400, natural color, gentle grain. No people, no text, no logos.

**A3: `a3-site-post`, square 1:1**
> A close, quiet photograph of a weathered wooden campsite number post at the edge of a pine-forest
> campsite at blue hour. The post's face is blank and smooth, with no numbers or letters. A picnic table
> and a dark tent are softly out of focus behind it, and a faint warm lantern glow is in the background.
> Cool blue light with one small warm accent. 35mm film look, shallow depth of field, natural color. No
> people, no text anywhere.

**A4: `a4-cta-morning`, wide 21:9 (or 16:9)**
> A wide, calm photograph of early morning at a lakeside campsite: mist lifting off still water, granite
> shoreline, dark pines on the far shore, first warm sunlight touching the treetops. In the near left
> foreground, an enamel camp mug steams on a flat rock. Most of the frame is soft, quiet water and mist,
> for text. 35mm film look, soft natural color, gentle grain. No people, no text, no logos.

## Direction B: "Trail poster" (painted)

Use these colors in every B prompt: deep pine green **#24382A**, forest green **#1E7A4C**, warm
ochre **#D9932B**, pale ochre **#FBF0DC**, cream paper **#F5F7F2**, slate blue **#2C4A8A**.

**B1: `b1-poster-wide`, landscape 3:2**
> A vintage American national-park travel poster painting, landscape 3:2, in the style of 1930s WPA
> screen-print posters: flat layered shapes, gouache texture, subtle paper grain, crisp edges. A
> red-tailed hawk soars over a granite valley with a winding river. Tall dark pines frame the right edge,
> and a small canvas tent glows warm beside the river in the lower middle. Layered mountains recede
> into the distance under a big, flat, calm sky that fills the upper-left half of the image, empty, for a
> headline. Strictly limited palette: deep pine green #24382A, forest green #1E7A4C, warm ochre
> #D9932B, pale ochre #FBF0DC, cream #F5F7F2, slate blue #2C4A8A. No text, no lettering, no border,
> no frame, no signature.

**B2: `b2-poster-tall`, portrait 2:3**
> The same vintage WPA-style national-park poster painting as a portrait 2:3 image: a red-tailed
> hawk soaring high over a granite valley and a river, tall pines in the lower corners, a small glowing
> tent by the river at the bottom. The upper half is a big, flat, calm sky, empty, for a headline. Flat
> layered shapes, gouache texture, subtle paper grain. Palette only: #24382A, #1E7A4C, #D9932B,
> #FBF0DC, #F5F7F2, #2C4A8A. No text, no lettering, no border.

**B3–B6: spot vignettes, square 1:1, one per message**
Start each with: *"A small vintage WPA-style screen-print vignette on a plain cream (#F5F7F2)
background, square, centered, flat layered shapes, gouache texture, palette only #24382A #1E7A4C
#D9932B #FBF0DC #2C4A8A, no text, no border:"* then add the subject:
- **`b3-watch`:** "…a brass pocket watch lying open on a pine bough, its hands at 12."
- **`b4-pack`:** "…a canvas backpack with a coiled rope and a tin cup, ready to go."
- **`b5-map`:** "…a folded trail map with a small ochre pin marking a campsite by a lake; no words or numbers on the map."
- **`b6-calendar`:** "…a paper wall calendar whose day squares are blank (no numbers), three of them circled in ochre, beside a lantern."
